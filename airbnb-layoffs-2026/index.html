⚠️ WARN Act Status — Airbnb
Airbnb is headquartered in San Francisco. California WARN notices were filed for affected employees. Airbnb has generally provided full WARN-compliant notice.
🛡️ If You Are 40 or Older — Federal Law Gives You Time
The Older Workers Benefit Protection Act gives workers 40 and older at least 21 days to review any severance agreement, and 45 days if this was a group layoff. You also have 7 days to revoke after signing. Airbnb cannot legally require you to sign immediately.

Airbnb 2026 Severance Package: Reported Terms

Airbnb's reported severance is a flat 14 weeks minimum for all employees regardless of tenure, which is one of the most generous floors in tech. The company covers 4 months of COBRA premiums and provides job placement support and immigration assistance for affected visa holders.

⚠️ Legally Required Consultation in Australia, EU & UK
Atlassian is headquartered in Australia with major U.S. operations (roughly 640 of the 1,600 roles cut are in North America). In Australia, the EU, and the UK, employers are legally required to complete a consultation process before finalizing terminations — which is why exit dates for this round are rolling out over months rather than all at once. If you're a U.S.-based employee, verify you received a WARN-compliant notice period for your state.
🛡️ If You Are 40 or Older — Federal Law Gives You Time
The Older Workers Benefit Protection Act gives workers 40 and older at least 21 days to review any severance agreement, and 45 days if this was a group layoff. You also have 7 days to revoke after signing. Your employer cannot legally require you to sign immediately.

About This Tool

Built for the Worst Week of Your Career

Getting laid off is disorienting. The first thing most people need is clarity on one question: how long can I survive? We built the tool to answer that — in under 60 seconds, for free, with no account required.

What LayoffSurvival does

LayoffSurvival is a free financial runway calculator built specifically for US workers who've been laid off. Enter your savings, monthly expenses, severance, and state — and the calculator shows you exactly how long your money will last, what your unemployment benefits will be, what COBRA will cost you, and a month-by-month breakdown of your financial situation.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. We don't collect your financial data, we don't require an account, and we don't sell anything. Your numbers stay on your device.

Why we built it

When you get laid off, the financial questions hit immediately — often before the shock has even worn off. How long can I pay my mortgage? Should I take COBRA or find a marketplace plan? When do I file for unemployment? Do I have enough runway to be selective about my next job?

Most people guess at these answers, or spend hours trying to piece together information from multiple sources. We wanted to build something that gave anyone in this situation real numbers in under a minute — not to replace a financial advisor, but to give people the clarity they need to make smart decisions fast.

How the estimates work

The unemployment benefit estimates are based on each state's published benefit formula — most states pay 40–60% of your prior weekly wage up to a state-specific maximum weekly benefit. The calculator uses 2026 state data for all 50 states.

COBRA cost estimates use the most recent Kaiser Family Foundation employer health benefits survey data — the most widely cited source for US health insurance premium benchmarks. The calculator shows both COBRA cost and an ACA marketplace estimate so you can compare.

Financial runway is calculated as total liquid resources (savings + severance + projected unemployment benefits) divided by your monthly expenses, shown month by month so you can see exactly when your situation changes.

All estimates are ranges, not guarantees. Your actual unemployment benefit depends on your specific wage history and state determination. This tool is for planning purposes and is not financial or legal advice.

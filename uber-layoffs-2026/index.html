⚠️ WARN Act Status — Uber
Uber's primary US offices are in San Francisco and New York. WARN Act notices have been filed in both California and New York for affected employees. Uber has generally met WARN Act notice requirements.
🛡️ If You Are 40 or Older — Federal Law Gives You Time
The Older Workers Benefit Protection Act gives workers 40 and older at least 21 days to review any severance agreement, and 45 days if this was a group layoff. You also have 7 days to revoke after signing. Uber cannot legally require you to sign immediately.

Uber 2026 Severance Package: Reported Terms

Uber has reported a flat 10-week minimum severance for corporate employees in recent rounds, with additional weeks for longer tenures. The company has covered 3 months of COBRA premiums. Outplacement support has been included in some packages.

RSU and equity situation Uber uses a 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff. Employees laid off before the cliff lose unvested shares. Post-cliff, Uber RSUs vest monthly, so even days before a monthly vest date represent real money worth discussing.

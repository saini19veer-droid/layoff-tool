LayoffSurvival.com provides a free online calculator designed to help individuals estimate their financial runway after a job loss. The calculator takes user-supplied inputs — including savings, monthly expenses, severance, and state of residence — and returns estimated figures including:

  • Estimated months of financial runway
  • Estimated unemployment benefit amounts by state
  • Estimated COBRA and ACA health insurance costs
  • A month-by-month financial timeline

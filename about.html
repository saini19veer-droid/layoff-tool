About LayoffSurvival

Built for the worst week of your career

LayoffSurvival is a free set of tools and guides built for one purpose: helping people who just lost their job understand their situation clearly, act quickly, and make their money last as long as possible.

Why this exists

Getting laid off is one of the most disorienting financial events in a person's life. The information you need — how long your money lasts, what your severance is worth, what you're legally entitled to, which expenses to cut first — is scattered across government websites, law firm blogs, and Reddit threads written by strangers with unknown agendas.

LayoffSurvival puts all of it in one place, in plain language, in tools that do the math for you. No email required. No account. No data stored on any server. Everything runs in your browser.

The tools are free because the people who need them most are the people who can least afford to pay for them right now.

Our principles

  • 🔒Fully private. Nothing you enter into any tool is stored, transmitted, or tracked. All calculations run in your browser. We have no idea who you are or what your situation is.
  • 🆓Free forever. Every tool, guide, and calculator is free. The site is supported by Google AdSense advertising. We do not sell data, require email signups, or hide content behind paywalls.
  • ✅Accurate, not alarmist. Severance data comes from WARN Act public filings, reported employee outcomes, and employment attorney guidance. We update pages when new information is available.
  • ⚖️Not legal or financial advice. The information here is for general educational purposes. For specific legal questions about your situation — especially before signing a severance agreement — consult a licensed employment attorney in your state. Many offer free initial consultations.

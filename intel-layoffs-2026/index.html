⚠️ WARN Act — Intel Filed in Multiple States, Oregon Law Is Stricter
Intel filed WARN Act notices in Oregon (Hillsboro), California (Santa Clara), and Arizona (Chandler). Oregon’s WARN Act has a lower threshold than federal law. If you received less than 60 days notice or equivalent pay, consult an employment attorney before signing your release of claims.
🛡️ If You Are 40 or Older — Take Your Full Review Period
Federal OWBPA law guarantees workers 40 and older 21 days to review any severance agreement — 45 days in group layoffs. You also have 7 days to revoke after signing. Given Intel’s below-average severance terms, taking the full review period is especially worthwhile.

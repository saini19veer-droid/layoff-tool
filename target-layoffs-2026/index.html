⚠️ First Major Layoff at Target in a Decade
Target's October 2025 cuts — about 1,000 layoffs plus roughly 800 unfilled positions eliminated, an estimated 8% reduction to its corporate workforce — marked the company's first major layoff round since 2015. Given the scale and Minnesota headquarters, affected employees in bulk locations were very likely covered by state WARN-style notice requirements. If you received less than the legally required notice for your state, it's worth having an employment attorney review your situation.
🛡️ If You Are 40 or Older — Federal Law Gives You Time
The Older Workers Benefit Protection Act gives workers 40 and older at least 21 days to review any severance agreement, and 45 days if this was a group layoff. You also have 7 days to revoke after signing. Your employer cannot legally require you to sign immediately.

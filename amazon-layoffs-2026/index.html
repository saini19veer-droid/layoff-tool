⚠️ WARN Act — Amazon Filed Notices, But Violations May Still Exist
Amazon filed WARN Act notices covering 2,198 corporate positions in Washington state starting April 2026, and additional notices in California, New Jersey, and Virginia. However, potential WARN Act violations are being investigated for Amazon Fresh employees in California, Maryland, and Pennsylvania following the January 28, 2026 store closures. If you worked at Amazon Fresh and received less than 60 days notice, you may be owed up to 60 days of back pay.
🛡️ If You Are 40 or Older — Federal Law Gives You Time
The Older Workers Benefit Protection Act gives workers 40 and older at least 21 days to review any severance agreement, and 45 days if this was a group layoff. You also have 7 days to revoke after signing. Amazon cannot legally require you to sign immediately.

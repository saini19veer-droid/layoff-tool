⚠️ Pinterest Has Not Disclosed Department-Level Layoff Details
Unusually for a layoff this size, Pinterest's leadership declined to share which teams or how many people specifically were cut in each department, citing employee privacy — and the company fired two engineers who built an internal tool to track affected colleagues. If you're trying to understand your severance relative to others in your situation, know that public information here is limited; lean on the Department of Labor's WARN Act database for your state and any documentation you personally received.
🛡️ If You Are 40 or Older — Federal Law Gives You Time
The Older Workers Benefit Protection Act gives workers 40 and older at least 21 days to review any severance agreement, and 45 days if this was a group layoff. You also have 7 days to revoke after signing. Your employer cannot legally require you to sign immediately.

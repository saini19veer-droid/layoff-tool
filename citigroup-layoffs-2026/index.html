⚠️ Global Cuts Spread Thin — WARN Coverage Varies by Location
Unlike a single-site plant closure, Citigroup's reductions are distributed across many offices and countries — including a prior round of roughly 3,500 positions eliminated at two China technology centers. That means individual U.S. locations may fall under or over WARN Act notice thresholds independently. If your role was based in a U.S. office with a sizeable local reduction, check whether you were entitled to 60 days' notice under federal or state WARN law.
🛡️ If You Are 40 or Older — Federal Law Gives You Time
The Older Workers Benefit Protection Act gives workers 40 and older at least 21 days to review any severance agreement, and 45 days if this was a group layoff. You also have 7 days to revoke after signing. Your employer cannot legally require you to sign immediately.
